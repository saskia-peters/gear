# History & Reports

Every inspection and every reinstatement is recorded. Depending on your
permission, you can:

- **View the per-tool history** — the full audit trail of one tool: every
  inspection (who, when, outcome, notes, the snapshotted checklist items) and
  every reinstatement (who, when, reason), newest first.
- **Export the report as a PDF** — the server-generated report is available to
  Führende/Admin holders.

## Why it matters

- **Audits are answered in seconds** — "was this tool inspected on time, by
  whom, and what was the result?" is one click away.
- **The history is append-only.** Records are never edited or deleted, so the
  trail is trustworthy.
- **DSGVO** — if a volunteer leaves, their personal references can be
  anonymized or deleted through the admin DSGVO operations, while the tool's
  history stays intact.
