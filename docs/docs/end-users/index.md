## The rules in one sentence

Every tool has exactly one status, **derived from real inspections** — and an
inspection is only allowed for someone who **holds the required qualification**.
That is the whole system: record what you did, and the app keeps the truth for
everyone.

## A quick tour

1. **Log in** with your email and password.
2. The **dashboard** shows all tools with their status.
3. A tool that is due (Orange or Red) is opened to **perform the inspection**.
4. The **result** (pass/fail or checklist) is recorded — and if it fails, the
   tool goes **out of service**.
5. **History** and **PDF reports** are available to those with the right
   permission.
